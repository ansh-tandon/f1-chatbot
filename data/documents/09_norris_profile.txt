LANDO NORRIS — DRIVER PROFILE

Full name: Lando Norris
Nationality: British
Date of birth: November 13, 1999
Car number: #4
Team: McLaren (since 2019)

2024 SEASON CONTEXT
Lando Norris was McLaren's lead driver entering the 2024 Monaco Grand Prix. The 2024 season had started strongly for McLaren, with the MCL38 proving competitive across multiple circuit types. Norris had secured his first Formula 1 victory in Miami in 2024, ahead of Monaco.

MONACO HISTORY
2022 Monaco GP: Norris finished in the points, demonstrating consistent performance.
2023 Monaco GP: Norris finished seventh after a difficult race.
2024 Monaco GP: Started fourth, finished seventh.

DRIVING STYLE
Norris is known for his raw one-lap pace and natural talent. He has a smooth and flowing driving style well-suited to medium and high-speed circuits. His Monaco performances have historically been solid but not spectacular — the circuit's low-speed character doesn't maximally exploit his strengths.

He is recognised as one of the most technically proficient drivers in terms of providing simulator and engineering feedback, which has contributed to McLaren's development during his tenure.

Norris on Monaco challenges: "Monaco is a very particular circuit. You need to be on it 100% from the first lap of qualifying. Every tenth matters there. I enjoy the challenge but it doesn't always bring out the best in our car."

His tyre management in 2024 was generally strong, though McLaren reported higher-than-expected degradation at Monaco specifically, related to the MCL38's front tyre behaviour in the stop-start braking zones.

CHAMPIONSHIP POSITION 2024
Norris entered Monaco 2024 in a strong championship position, building on his Miami victory. The Monaco result (seventh, 6 points) was a setback but he remained competitive in the overall standings, targeting circuit types where the MCL38 showed stronger pace.
