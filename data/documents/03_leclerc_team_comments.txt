SOURCE: Ferrari Official Team Statement
TITLE: Leclerc Post-Race Team Debrief Comments - Monaco 2024
DATE: 2024-05-26
DRIVER: LEC
TEAM: Ferrari
RACE: Monaco 2024
SESSION: Race

Charles Leclerc, Ferrari driver, speaking after his Monaco 2024 victory:

"This win means the world to me. Monaco is my home race and I have waited so long for this. We did everything perfectly today — the team, the strategy, the pit stop. I am so proud."

"The key was the first stint. I managed the tyres perfectly on the mediums and kept the gap consistent. When the safety car came out, we made the right call to pit immediately. I knew that could be the decisive moment of the race."

"The hard tyres were great in the second stint. The degradation was very manageable and I could push when I needed to without worrying too much about the rubber."

"I've always said I wanted to win here at home more than anywhere else. This is for Monaco, this is for the fans, this is for the team that believed in me."

Ferrari Team Principal Fred Vasseur:
"Charles drove a perfect race. The strategy call under the safety car was critical — our guys on the pit wall and in the garage reacted instantly. Charles never put a wheel wrong. This is a very emotional win for the whole team."

Ferrari Strategy Director on the one-stop approach:
"We analyzed all scenarios before the race. Monaco is unusual because track position is so valuable — it's nearly impossible to overtake. We committed to a one-stop early and the car's pace gave us confidence we could manage the tyres to the end. When the safety car came out at lap 27, we brought Charles in immediately. That was the decisive move."
