CHARLES LECLERC — DRIVER PROFILE

Full name: Charles Marc Hervé Perceval Leclerc
Nationality: Monegasque
Date of birth: October 16, 1997
Car number: #16
Team: Scuderia Ferrari (since 2019)

MONACO HISTORY
Charles Leclerc was born and raised in Monaco. The Monaco Grand Prix has always been the most significant race of his career aspirations. He grew up watching the race from the barriers as a child and learned to drive on Monaco's roads.

2022 Monaco GP: Leclerc started on pole position but was the victim of a dramatic Ferrari strategy error. A pit stop miscommunication left him stationary in the pit lane for too long, dropping from the lead to fourth. A devastating result that highlighted his Monaco misfortune.

2023 Monaco GP: Leclerc again started from pole position but was unable to convert it into a victory. He finished second behind Max Verstappen, hampered by tyre strategy decisions and the difficulty of overtaking at Monaco.

2024 Monaco GP: VICTORY. His third consecutive Monaco pole finally converted into a win. Leclerc became the first Monegasque driver to win their home race since Louis Chiron in 1931. The win ended years of near-misses and heartbreak at his home circuit.

DRIVING STYLE
Leclerc is known for his exceptional one-lap pace and ability to extract maximum performance in qualifying. At Monaco, his intimate knowledge of every centimetre of the circuit — learned since childhood — gives him a significant mental advantage. He is particularly strong in the final sector and shows exceptional car placement through the Swimming Pool complex.

His race craft involves smooth tyre management and consistent, controlled pace. He rarely makes mistakes under pressure and excels at maintaining concentration over long stints.

2024 SEASON CONTEXT
Entering Monaco 2024, Leclerc was third in the Drivers' Championship. Ferrari's SF-24 showed strong performance on low-speed circuits, giving Leclerc confidence heading into Monaco. His two consecutive Monaco poles demonstrated the car's suitability and his personal mastery of the circuit.
