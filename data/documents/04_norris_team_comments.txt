SOURCE: McLaren Official Team Statement
TITLE: Norris Post-Race Team Comments - Monaco 2024
DATE: 2024-05-26
DRIVER: NOR
TEAM: McLaren
RACE: Monaco 2024
SESSION: Race

Lando Norris, McLaren driver, after finishing seventh at Monaco 2024:

"It was a tough race. We didn't have the pace we hoped for today. Charles was in a different league, honestly. He was untouchable."

"Our strategy didn't quite work out the way we wanted. The safety car timing wasn't ideal for us — we were caught out a bit. But I don't think we had the pace to win regardless today."

"I felt the degradation was quite high on my tyres compared to what we expected. The mediums in my opening stint didn't feel as good as in practice. The pace just wasn't there."

"Monaco is a street circuit where everything is about qualifying and track position. When you start fourth you need things to go perfectly and they didn't for us today. We'll look at the data and come back stronger."

McLaren Team Principal Andrea Stella:
"Today wasn't our day. Lando drove well in difficult circumstances. The safety car deployment caught us at a disadvantaged moment strategically. We'll review everything and take the learnings forward."

"Our pace compared to Ferrari today shows they had a genuine advantage on this circuit. We need to understand the tyre degradation issues we experienced — it was higher than predicted in our models. Lando managed it as well as he could but it cost us lap time throughout the race."

McLaren Strategy Update:
"Our initial plan was to run a longer first stint but the tyre behaviour forced us to adapt. We had hoped for more from our second stint on the hard compound but pace was below expectation. Monaco has specific characteristics — its abrasive surface and the stop-start nature put unique stress on the tyres."
